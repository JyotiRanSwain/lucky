Data Security

Privacy Policy

At Lucky Diagnostics, we are committed to safeguarding your medical data and protecting your personal privacy.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver diagnostic tests, home sample collections, and health reports, we may collect the following personal details:

  • Personal Identification: Name, age, gender, and contact details (phone number, email address).
  • Address Information: Home or delivery address provided for home sample collection services.
  • Medical & Clinical Data: Test requisitions, doctor prescriptions, and test result data required for diagnostic evaluation.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide healthcare services and ensure accurate diagnostic results.

  • Scheduling phlebotomists for doorstep sample collection.
  • Processing diagnostic tests and generating verified health reports.
  • Delivering test reports via secure WhatsApp links, email, or physical copies.
  • Communicating critical appointment or medical updates.

3. Data Confidentiality & Security

We implement stringent technical and organizational measures to ensure your health information remains strictly confidential.

  • Diagnostic reports and patient profiles are restricted to authorized laboratory technicians and clinical specialists.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or medical data to third-party marketing companies.
  • Data is stored on secure, encrypted infrastructure with regular access audits.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We only share patient data under specific and limited circumstances:

  • With accredited partner laboratories or referral specialists when specific clinical expertise is required.
  • When explicitly requested by you or your treating doctor.
  • When mandatory under Indian regulatory laws or health authority orders.
